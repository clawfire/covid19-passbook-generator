The end of the road

Covid19-Passbook is now closed. You can no longer generate a pass from this website.

In June 2021, there was no simple way to carry an EU Digital COVID Certificate on an iPhone without installing yet another app. So we built one that didn't need installing: scan your QR code, get a pass for Apple Wallet, and keep your data on your phone.

The world has moved on, and so has this project. The EU Digital COVID Certificate regulation expired in mid-2023, and the Apple certificate used to sign the passes has expired too. Without a valid signature, Wallet would refuse the passes anyway. Keeping a broken generator online would only waste people's time, so it's time to close the door properly.

What does this mean for you?

  1. Passes already in your Wallet are not affected by the shutdown. They are files on your phone; nothing was ever stored on our side.
  2. The signing service has been deleted. No server, no data, nothing left running.
  3. The source code stays public on GitHub, for history, curiosity, or your next project.

Fin de l’aventure

En juin 2021, il n’existait aucun moyen simple de garder son certificat COVID numérique de l’UE sur iPhone sans installer une énième application. Sans signature valide, Wallet refuserait de toute façon les pass. Laisser en ligne un générateur cassé ne ferait que vous faire perdre du temps : place à une fermeture en bonne et due forme.

Qu’est-ce que ça change pour vous ?
